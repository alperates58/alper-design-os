# Knowledge Architecture: Domain Research & Discovery

Operational taxonomy of software domains and industry operational profiles.

## Industry Taxonomy
- **Industrial & Manufacturing Operations**: Resource allocations, shift scheduling, machine telemetries, bottleneck tracking.
- **Enterprise Resource Planning & Ledgers**: Multi-currency transactions, double-entry ledgers, auditing compliance, inventory reconciliation.
- **Clinical & Healthcare Workbenches**: Patient timelines, laboratory specimens, diagnostic telemetry, HIPAA/GDPR strict auditing.
- **Financial Trading & Risk**: Order books, market depth, tick charts, latency-sensitive order entry.
- **Field & Logistics Operations**: Vehicle telematics, route optimization, real-time map trackers, geofencing.
- **Cybersecurity & Threat Telemetry**: Incident response queues, network anomaly alerts, security event graphs.
